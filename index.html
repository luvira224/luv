Compatibility reference

Changing your hair color recalculates the scores of every submitted color. Your existing manual decisions are retained.

The score measures color-theory compatibility, not readability or accessibility contrast. It combines hue relationship, saturation balance, lightness contrast, relative luminance, and CIE Lab color difference.
A submitted color that scores at or above your threshold is initially marked ALLOWED. You can override any decision at any time.
